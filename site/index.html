The question

How do conversations, stored memories, and queries relate in embedding space?

01 What we study

We embed conversation turns, stored memory texts, and queries in a shared space. We measure query proximity, memory–conversation correspondence, and conversation structure.

A supervised classifier uses 25 features to predict answer correctness before generation. We evaluate four dense or hybrid retrieval implementations and one observation-summary comparator.

02 What we observe

The associations vary by question type. On LongMemEval-small, geometry reaches 0.640 AUROC across 2,367 question–system pairs, compared with 0.655 for a question-difficulty baseline.

Its strongest category score is 0.841 for single-session-assistant questions. System identity alone reaches 0.826 on the same category.

03 What the controls show

After controlling for question difficulty and system identity, geometry adds no statistically established pooled improvement.

ΔAUROC +0.002
95% CI [−0.010, 0.014]

Embedding proximity does not verify fact coverage or distinguish construction, retrieval, and generation errors.
