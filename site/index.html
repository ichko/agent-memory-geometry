The question

How do conversations, stored memories, and queries relate in embedding space?

01 What we study

We embed conversation turns, memory texts, and queries with a shared analysis model. We measure query proximity, memory–conversation correspondence, and conversation structure.

We test 25 pre-answer features with a classifier trained on correctness labels. We evaluate four dense or hybrid retrieval implementations and one observation-summary comparator.

02 What we observe

In our offline LongMemEval-small evaluation, geometry reaches a modest 0.640 AUROC across 2,367 question–system pairs. The question-difficulty baseline reaches 0.655.

Its strongest category score is 0.841 for single-session-assistant questions. System identity alone reaches 0.826 on the same category.

03 What the controls show

After controlling for question difficulty and system identity, geometry adds no statistically established pooled improvement.

ΔAUROC +0.002
95% CI [−0.010, 0.014]

This study does not establish a reliable live failure detector or identify why individual answers fail.
